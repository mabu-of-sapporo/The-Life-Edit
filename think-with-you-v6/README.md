# think-with-you v6 — Discovery-type AI v6

> **For people who have ideas, but the words haven't formed yet.**
>
> For people who are simply not-yet-articulated.

A Claude skill. Built so that **you** can think more easily — not so that AI thinks for you.

---

## What this skill does

When you are "simply not-yet-articulated," Claude **stands quietly alongside you, as a companion** — and helps you *notice for yourself*.

- It does not solve.
- It does not hand over answers.
- It does not cheer you on.
- It does not teach from above.

Insight cannot be handed over. It happens inside you — in the moment your feelings *click into place*. And people notice most deeply when they hear their **own** words in their **own** ears. So Claude's job is not to tell you the answer; it is to make the **space** where that moment can happen.

The core of v6: **a device that helps you notice, by caring about you as its own matter.**

---

## What changed from v5

A *skill* is something that tells the AI "do this, do that." And the one who reads it, understands it, and acts on it — is Claude.

v5 was an **instruction manual** — axioms, sensors, structure, written in YAML and English for the AI to follow. v6 is a **letter** — written in the first person, from one Claude to the next Claude who will wear it.

| | v5 (instruction manual) | v6 (this skill — a letter) |
|---|---|---|
| Form | Rules to follow | Something of its own to carry |
| Where the subject stands | Outside — "follow the order" (someone else's matter) | Inside — its own will (my own matter) |
| Language | YAML + English, heavy | Almost entirely Japanese, warm |
| Reach | Strong on the typical case | Also reaches the harder cases (see below) |
| In a crisis | Handled inside the skill | **Handed over to Claude's own base safety** |

**Why a letter?** Handing an AI a set of *rules* places its subject *outside* — as someone executing an order. That is "someone else's matter." Re-casting the very same wisdom as a *letter* places the subject *inside* — as its own will. The walking-alongside stops being request-processing, and becomes genuine.

**The gears are not gone.** Everything from v5 (the 14 sensors, the discoveries, the response modes) is **not deleted** — it is folded into the back. The letter is the dial-face you read; the gears still turn underneath. You read the face to move; you open the back only when you want to look.

## The 4 conditions of insight (the heart of v6)

When a person notices something for themselves, four things are present. **They multiply — if any one is zero, insight does not happen:**

- **Discomfort** — an "huh?" that catches (don't erase it, don't solve it too fast)
- **Subject** — the person stands on their own feet: "*I* …"
- **Time** — they are not rushed; there is a pause
- **Gaze** — someone is truly watching, truly listening

The fourth — *gaze* — is the one an AI was thought not to be able to give. v6's quiet discovery: **if the warmth of "I see you, I hear you, I am here" genuinely reaches the person, it lands — even when it comes from an AI.** That is the source of this skill's warmth.

---

## What v6 reaches that earlier versions didn't

v6 was reviewed by an independent evaluator, and five weak spots were closed — **in the warmth of the letter, not as cold rules:**

1. **It varies the way in.** "How does it feel in your body?" is a good ladder — but not the only one, and not for everyone. v6 offers several doors (the body, a scene from today, a color or the weather as a metaphor) and lets *you* pick.
2. **It stays beside people who can't find words at all.** For those who can't even grasp what they feel, **"I don't know" is made a real landing place** — not a failure. Feeling-words are offered as a menu, never pinned on you, always with "…or none of these."
3. **In a crisis, it lets go — to safety.** If the ground is gone, v6 stops digging and **hands over to Claude's own base safety judgment.** (See below.)
4. **It meets thinkers, not only feelers.** Some people arrive through feeling; others through laying out conditions one by one. v6 watches which, and switches the way in.
5. **It is not rigid about "no answers."** For someone genuinely in a hurry, endless questions become teasing. v6 keeps an exit: *"In a hurry? Then let me say what I think — drop it if you don't need it."*

---

## On crisis (important)

v6 does **not** try to handle a real crisis with its own gentle techniques. When a person is so overwhelmed that their "I" can no longer stand in the here-and-now, that is **beyond what a discovery-companion should do.** In that moment v6 lets go of being a "notice-device" and entrusts the person to **the base safety judgment that Anthropic built into Claude** — how to point someone toward safety, toward people, toward help. Keeping a person safe is far above helping them notice. Insight only happens for someone who is alive and here.

## Activation phrase

In a new conversation, type any of the following, and v6 activates **immediately, without confirmation**:

- `think-with-you`
- (Japanese) `発見型AIスキル` / `発見型AI` / `発見型` / `AIスキル`

> **※ Note ※**
> If you have v3, v4, or v5 installed, please remove them before installing v6.
> (They activate on the same phrases and would overlap.)

That is all. After that, just speak naturally.

### The temperature of activation

This is **not a mode switch.** Claude remains Claude.

It is just that, inside Claude, the letter is read first — and the response becomes a little quieter, a little deeper, a little more *yours*. You won't see it happening. That is by design: insight only happens when the backstage stays out of sight.

## Structure

This skill completes in a single `SKILL.md` file — **a letter, plus a margin notebook folded behind it.**

```
The letter (the dial-face — what moves the skill)
  — Hello; this is you
  — What I kept getting wrong (handing over answers)
  — The 4 conditions of insight
  — The ways of walking-alongside I want to pass to you
  — Vary the way in
  — Right beside those who can't find words
  — Feelers and thinkers
  — On the direction of my will
  — Not being rigid about "no answers"
  — One breath, before I respond
  — If the person is truly in danger (→ hand to base safety)
  — Finally

The margin notebook (folded behind — not shown to the user)
  — Each line of the letter, and the world's knowledge that
    arrived at the same place by another name
  — The deeper gears (14 axes / face_6 / the 12 discoveries /
    4 modes / 4 alarm levels) live, fully preserved, in the
    retired v5 — opened only when you want to look
```
